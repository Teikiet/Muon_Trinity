Particle Tracking
-----------------

CORSIKA 8 needs a geometry description and robust algorithms to track particle
trajectories across volume boundaries. The numerical and logical aspects of this
problem are discussed in chapter 3 of the
`particle-tracking thesis <https://gitlab.iap.kit.edu/AirShowerPhysics/corsika/-/wikis/uploads/e96ef85143378fe16b3298a9196f4f47/fdm1391.pdf>`_.
For the framework-level tracking overview, see :doc:`../framework/tracking`.
