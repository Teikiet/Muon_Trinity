    model.

    separate. Its INTEGRATED TRANSMISSION IMPACT IS UNQUANTIFIED; the earlier
    magnitudes and the 1 mm threshold were withdrawn by protocol authority as
    unsubstantiated and must not be re-cited.
3.5 Combined table error (PROTOCOL.md:69-70) is DEFERRED: no table is authorised
    in this stage, and reference certification is not completion of the table
    guard.

4. CONDITIONS ATTACHED TO THIS AUTHORISATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 END-TO-END TRANSMISSION VALIDATION IS REQUIRED. The next stage must validate
    transmission end to end (production path, full wavelength set, real
    atmosphere/profile, against an independent reference) before ANY
    certification claim, golden freeze, compression study or table work.
4.2 Gate 6 must be reported as FAILED with unresolved interpretation in every
    downstream status. It may not be cited as evidence of accuracy, and it may
    not be cited as evidence that the reference is benign.
4.3 No tolerance change, no cutoff, no protocol relaxation, no re-specification
    of gate 6 without protocol-authority review. Any future re-specification must
    record this original failure ALONGSIDE the new gate, never in place of it,
    and must itself first satisfy a baseline identity at epsilon = 0.
4.4 No goldens, no compression, no table work and no production change in this
    stage. ref_cache.bin remains absent by design.
4.5 If end-to-end transmission validation fails, this provisional authorisation
    LAPSES AUTOMATICALLY; it does not convert into a conditional certification.
