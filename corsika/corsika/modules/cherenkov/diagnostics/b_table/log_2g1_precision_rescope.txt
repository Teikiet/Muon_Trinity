1. ADOPTED ACCEPTANCE TARGET (replaces the precision programme)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 End-to-end production transmission is judged against ONE target:
        |T_prod - T_ref|                <= 1.0e-03   (absolute), AND
        |T_prod - T_ref| / T_ref        <= 0.5e-02    wherever T_ref > 0.05.
1.2 The proposed numbers are CONFIRMED UNCHANGED by the executing agent: no
    tightening and no relaxation. They are ~10x tighter than the smallest
    relevant atmospheric systematic.
1.3 Rationale (recorded, not re-derived here): total energy-scale error for
    current IACTs is ~10-15% (CTA targets <10%); the atmospheric-transmission
    share is a few percent. Gates at 1e-10 are 8-9 orders of magnitude tighter
    than any IACT measurement.
